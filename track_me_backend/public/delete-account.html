Delete Your Account

Request permanent deletion of your Track Me account and all associated data.

⚠️ Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted

  • Your profile information (name, email, avatar)
  • All habits and completion history
  • All goals and progress records
  • All cash flow records, income, and expenses
  • All debt tracking records
  • AI-generated reports and analytics
  • Notification history and device registrations
  • Authentication tokens and sessions

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete From the App (Recommended)

1

Open Track Me

Launch the Track Me app on your device.

2

Go to Profile

Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.

3

Find Delete Account

Scroll to the "Account & Security" section and tap "Delete Account".

4

Confirm Deletion

Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm permanent deletion of your account.

Can't Access the App?

If you are unable to access the Track Me app, you can request account deletion by providing the email address associated with your account. We will send a verification email to confirm your identity before processing the deletion.

What Happens After Deletion

  • You will be immediately logged out of the app on all devices.
  • All your data will be permanently removed from our servers.
  • You will no longer receive any notifications from Track Me.
  • You can create a new account at any time, but your previous data cannot be recovered.
